Privacy Policy for Compass

App Name: Compass  |  Package Name: com.aistudio.truenorth.kpqzxm
Effective Date: November 20, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Compass Android application (package ID: com.aistudio.truenorth.kpqzxm) handles information when you use the app. We respect your privacy and designed Compass so that core compass functionality works without requiring an account or transmitting your personal information to any developer-owned servers.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

a) Foreground Location Data (Optional)

Compass requests optional foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) strictly while the app is open on your screen to calculate and display your current Latitude, Longitude, and GPS Altitude using Google Play Services.

  • Location is used only for real-time on-screen display inside the app.
  • Compass does not access background location when the app is closed.
  • The developer does not collect, log, sell, or store your GPS coordinates on any external server.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android Settings; the compass dial and magnetic sensor features continue to work without location access.
b) Device Motion & Magnetic Sensors (On-Device Only)

Compass reads hardware sensor data (rotation vector, accelerometer, and magnetometer) and uses optional device vibration (VIBRATE) to render heading angles, tilt compensation, magnetic field strength (µT), interference/calibration alerts, and optional directional haptic ticks. All sensor readings are processed locally in real time on your device and are never stored or shared.

c) Advertising & Network Data (Google AdMob)

Compass uses network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load advertisements via Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device or Other Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), app set ID, or similar identifiers.
  • Diagnostics & Interaction Data: Ad impressions, clicks, crash/performance metrics, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Approximate Location: General region/country inferred from IP address by Google for ad delivery and compliance.

2. Third-Party Services

Compass relies on the following third-party Google services, which process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Google Privacy Policy  |  How Google Uses Advertising Data
  • Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider): Google Privacy Policy

3. Data Sharing, Retention, and Security

  • No Developer Servers: Compass does not require user registration, accounts, or logins, and the developer does not collect or retain personal user data on private servers.
  • Third-Party Processing: Data processed by Google AdMob is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS encryption and retained according to Google's data retention policies.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: The developer does not sell your personal information.

4. Your Privacy Controls & Choices

  • Location Permission: Enable or revoke location access anytime under Android Settings > Apps > Compass > Permissions.
  • Advertising ID & Personalized Ads: Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized advertising, under Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google Settings > Ads).
  • Haptic Feedback: Toggle directional haptic vibrations off anytime inside the app's Settings sheet.

5. Children's Privacy

Compass is a general-utility navigation tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, legal requirements, or Google Play policy updates. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
